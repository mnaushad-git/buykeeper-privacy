This policy explains how BuyKeeper handles information. BuyKeeper is an offline purchase organizer: it helps you keep track of purchases, receipts, return deadlines and warranty expiry dates on your own Android device.

Summary

  • No account. BuyKeeper has no sign-up, sign-in or user profile.
  • No internet access. BuyKeeper does not request Android's internet permission, so the app cannot open network connections. It has no server, and Naushad Apps does not receive any information you enter into it.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. BuyKeeper contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • On-device storage. What you enter is stored in BuyKeeper's private storage on your device.
  • One exception you control: Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include your purchase list and BuyKeeper settings in your device backup. Receipt images are always excluded. See Android backup and device transfer.

BuyKeeper stores only what you enter, together with the details it needs to work:

BuyKeeper does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal profile information. It does not read device identifiers. Anything personal you type into a field such as notes (for example a serial number) is stored only as described in this policy.

Receipt images

  • How you choose an image. When you attach or replace a receipt, BuyKeeper opens Android's system photo picker, limited to images. You choose one image, and BuyKeeper can read only that image. On older Android versions without the photo picker, Android shows its own file chooser instead. BuyKeeper does not request access to your photo library, files, storage or camera, and it cannot take photos.
  • What BuyKeeper keeps. BuyKeeper reads the image you chose once and saves its own copy in its private app storage. The copy is resized and re-saved as a JPEG image. This re-saving removes the original photo's embedded metadata, including any GPS location. BuyKeeper does not keep a link to the original photo, and it never changes or deletes the original.
  • Who can see it. The copy is stored in app-private storage, which Android does not let other apps read. BuyKeeper does not share receipt images with other apps and does not upload them anywhere.
  • Backup. Receipt images are excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer.
  • Removal. When you remove or replace a receipt, or delete its purchase, BuyKeeper deletes its copy of the image. BuyKeeper also cleans up any leftover image copies when it starts, such as an image picked for a purchase that was never saved.

Reminders and notifications

  • Reminders are optional. They remind you before a return deadline or warranty expiry date.
  • Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by Android's job scheduler (WorkManager). They are not sent from a server, and they keep working after a restart without any network.
  • On Android 13 and later, Android asks for your permission before BuyKeeper can show notifications. BuyKeeper asks only after you choose to turn reminders on. You can turn reminders off in BuyKeeper, or switch notifications off in Android settings, at any time.
  • A reminder shows the product name and how soon the deadline is. On a secure lock screen, it shows only "BuyKeeper reminder: A deadline is coming up".
  • Once shown, notifications are handled by Android. Anyone who can see your unlocked screen, apps you have allowed to read your notifications, and connected devices such as smartwatches may be able to see reminder text.

Android backup and device transfer

BuyKeeper does not have its own backup or sync service, and it does not copy your data to any server.

BuyKeeper allows Android's own backup feature. If you have turned on backup for your device, Android may include the following in your backup or when you move to a new device:

  • Included: your purchase list (product names, categories, stores, prices, dates, notes and reminder choices) and your BuyKeeper settings (theme and default reminders).
  • Never included: receipt images, and BuyKeeper's record of whether it has already asked for notification permission.

This backup is created, stored and controlled by Android and your Google account, under Google's terms and privacy policy. Naushad Apps cannot access it. You can turn Android backup on or off, and manage or delete backups, in your device settings and Google account.

If you restore BuyKeeper on a new device, your purchases return without their receipt images. BuyKeeper marks those receipts as unavailable and lets you add the image again.

BuyKeeper does not request internet access, camera, microphone, location, contacts, accounts, phone, SMS, or broad storage or media access. It does not use the advertising ID.

Third-party services

BuyKeeper contains no third-party services that receive your information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social media or payment SDKs, and it does not use Firebase or Google Play services.

BuyKeeper is built with Google's open-source Android Jetpack libraries (Compose, Room, WorkManager, DataStore, Navigation, Lifecycle and ExifInterface) and the Kotlin standard libraries. These libraries run inside the app on your device, and they do not send data anywhere.

Google Play, which you use to install BuyKeeper, and Android itself have their own privacy practices, which are covered by Google's privacy policy rather than this one.

Sharing and selling

Naushad Apps does not receive your BuyKeeper information. That means Naushad Apps cannot share it, sell it, rent it or use it for advertising. BuyKeeper has no feature to share or export your data to other apps. The only route by which BuyKeeper data can leave your device is Android backup and device transfer, which you control (see above).

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it:

  • One purchase: delete it in BuyKeeper. This also deletes its receipt image and cancels its reminders.
  • One receipt: remove it from its purchase in BuyKeeper.
  • Everything: open Android Settings → Apps → BuyKeeper → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall BuyKeeper. This deletes all BuyKeeper data from the device.
  • Android backup copies: manage or delete them in your device's backup settings or your Google account.

Naushad Apps holds no copy of your information, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. If you contact us, we can explain how to delete data from your device.

Children's privacy

BuyKeeper is a general-purpose purchase organizer and is not directed at children. Naushad Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because BuyKeeper does not send information to us.

Security

  • BuyKeeper's database, settings and receipt images are kept in app-private storage, which Android isolates from other apps.
  • BuyKeeper exposes no data to other apps, through file sharing or otherwise.
  • Reminder notifications hide the product name on a secure lock screen.
  • BuyKeeper's own diagnostic logging is switched off in release versions, and even in test versions it never records product names, prices, notes or receipt contents.

BuyKeeper does not add its own password or encryption on top of Android's protections. Anyone who can unlock your device can open BuyKeeper and see your purchases. Use a screen lock to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If BuyKeeper's handling of information changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs internet access, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date before that version is released. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
